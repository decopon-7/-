Illustrative scale: 1 sim unit ≈ 14 cm, mohair-filled at a gentle density. Volume and energy are summed live over the body and every arm segment.

Inside the experiment

The coat is drawn as stacked fur shells over a soft body. Each arm is a chain of verlet particles with length constraints, pulled toward a resting curl by springs. Combing tilts the shells; a finger draws nearby arm tips in to hold on.
